About ChargeWise

Empowering Electric Mobility Across India

ChargeWise was built with a single mission: eliminate EV range anxiety by providing intelligent, real-time route optimization and battery simulation for electric vehicle owners.

Whether you're commuting across NCR or driving from Mumbai to Goa, ChargeWise calculates accurate energy consumption, compatible charger networks (CCS2, Type 2 AC), and optimal charging breaks before you step into your car.
